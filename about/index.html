About

Becoming, always.

“Banyuhay” means metamorphosis or transformation. The word has stayed with me because my path through mathematics has repeatedly changed form while keeping the same underlying curiosity.

I am John Rick Manzanares, a Filipino mathematician and computational researcher from Baguio City in the northern Philippines. I studied mathematics at the University of the Philippines Baguio after attending Philippine Science High School – Cordillera Administrative Region Campus.

My early interests were closer to differential geometry, but my research direction changed after I encountered topological data analysis. My master’s work studied homology-based centrality, and later projects brought topology into electrocardiogram classification, computer vision, and scientific data analysis.

I am now a Marie Skłodowska-Curie doctoral fellow working in Poland. My doctoral research focuses on mathematical and computational descriptions of bone microstructure, combining topological data analysis, computational geometry, scientific computing, and machine learning.

I also care about teaching and open research software. I like mathematical explanations that define their objects carefully, code that can be reviewed rather than treated as a black box, and tools that remain useful beyond a single experiment.
