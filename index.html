Mathematician · Computational researcher

Shape, structure, and change.

I am John Rick Manzanares, a Filipino mathematician working across topology, geometry, scientific computing, and machine learning.

My doctoral research studies bone microstructure using mathematical and computational tools, with an emphasis on interpretable descriptors of complex three-dimensional structure.

Selected research

Mathematics that meets structure.

The idea behind this site

Banyuhay

Banyuhay is a Filipino word for metamorphosis or transformation. It is a useful description of both mathematics and a life in research: ideas change, questions become sharper, and the path rarely remains what it was at the beginning.

This site gathers the parts of that continuing transformation: research, software, teaching, mathematical notes, and occasional writing about academic life.
